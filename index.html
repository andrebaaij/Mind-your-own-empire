About

You are a Mind, arrived on a new world to prepare it for the people who will follow. Your drones mine and build for you; one day they will have to deal with the original inhabitants and other interested parties, too.

This is an early prototype that started life in 2014 as a 2D side project. Explore the procedurally generated terrain, send drones over the hills, mine iron and raise solar arrays. Tell @andrebaaij what you think, or have a look at the code on GitHub.
